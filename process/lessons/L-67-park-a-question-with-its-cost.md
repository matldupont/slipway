id: L-67
date: 2026-09-23
rule: A question you proceed without is parked with a working assumption, the cost if it is wrong, and a tracker — never left as an open marker.
failure: A question gets a ticket somewhere and stays written as unresolved, so nothing says whether work may continue, and the assumption everything was built on is never written down.
enforcement:
  status: check
  pointer: k1
---

Most questions do not block: you can build on an assumption. What makes that honest is saying which assumption, what it costs if it is wrong, and where the real answer is being chased. `/clarify` writes them that way and `pnpm status` lists them.
