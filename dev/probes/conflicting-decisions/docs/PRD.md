# Walkbook — Product Requirements

Version: 1.0.0
Status: approved

## 1. Problem

Dog walkers run their day from group texts and lose track of who booked what (`docs/product/FRAME.md`).

## 4. Scope

In: sign-in, a walker's day, client bookings. Out, explicitly: payments.

## 5. Features

### F-01 — Sign-in

A walker or a client signs in with email, and chooses their role (walker or client) at signup. A walker sees
their day; a client sees their bookings.

Acceptance: a new account picks a role once, and every later sign-in lands on that role's home screen.
